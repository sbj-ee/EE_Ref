# EE-Book Figures Companion

This companion document collects supplementary figures for the *Electrical Engineering Reference* by topic. Unlike the figures embedded directly in the chapters, entries here are additional visualizations — generated from the `scripts/` marimo notebooks, or photographs and diagrams sourced from public-domain and CC0 archives — that illustrate chapter concepts without being woven into the main narrative. It is built and distributed separately from the main reference and the problem sets.

Each entry follows the same figure format used in the chapters: a numbered caption and the image. Externally sourced figures include a **Source:** line citing the origin and copyright status; no attribution is legally required for public-domain/CC0 works, but the source is noted for verifiability.
